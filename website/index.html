What the lights mean

  • Say hi

    Someone's here and open to talk. Walk over and join.

  • Free table

    Nobody's claimed it. Claim it to start your own.

  • Not now

    Taken, or not available.

How it works

  1. Scan in at the kiosk.
  2. See the floor map of tables.
  3. Find a green table to join, or claim a yellow one to start your own.
  4. Walk over and say hi.
  5. Stuck for words? A Gemini icebreaker gets you going.
  6. After the chat, rate it. If you both want to meet again, you swap socials. Report or block is always one tap away.

What you get

  • A kiosk with a live floor map.
  • A light box on each table.
  • Events for groups.
  • A dashboard with totals only, never who.

How to join

  1. Contact us.
  2. We set up the boxes.
  3. Place your tables on the floor map.
  4. Set your hours. Lights switch off after 10pm.
